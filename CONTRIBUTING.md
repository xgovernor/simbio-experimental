Contributing to SIMBIOThank you for your interest in contributing to SIMBIO! While SIMBIO is currently developed as a closed-source project, we value feedback and contributions from the community in specific areas.How You Can ContributeEven though direct code contributions via pull requests are not accepted at this time, you can contribute in the following ways:Reporting Bugs:If you encounter a bug or unexpected behavior, please check the issue tracker to see if it has already been reported.If not, please create a new issue, following the guidelines in our ISSUE_GUIDE.md. Provide as much detail as possible, including steps to reproduce, expected behavior, and actual behavior.Suggesting Features:Have an idea for a new feature or an improvement to an existing one? We'd love to hear it!Please check the issue tracker for existing feature requests.If your idea is new, create a new issue using the "Feature Request" template. Explain the feature, its benefits, and potential use cases.Improving Documentation:If you find errors, omissions, or areas for clarification in our documentation, please let us know.Create a new issue detailing the documentation problem and your suggested improvement.Providing Feedback:General feedback on your experience using SIMBIO is always welcome. You can share your thoughts by creating an issue or contacting us via the channels listed in SUPPORT.md.Code of ConductAll contributors and participants are expected to adhere to our Code of Conduct. Please ensure you read and understand it.Questions?If you have questions about contributing, feel free to reach out via the methods described in our SUPPORT.md file.Thank you for helping make SIMBIO better!
